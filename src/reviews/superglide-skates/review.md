# Pulsar Superglide Glass Skates

This was my first aftermarket skates that I've ever purchased for my first "proper" gaming mouse, the Razer Viper Ultimate. I opted for the white colour as my mouse was black at the time but its not like it matters as you won't ever see it unless you flip your mouse. My stock skates had worn down and I thought buying these would save me money because glass = no wear. Turns out, theres a reason why the skates take most of the wear and tear instead of your pad.

## First Impressions

Since these are glass build quality is of course extremely solid. These things will not wear except for the coating on the glass itself. They felt nice and made my mouse feel like it was on ice skates. For reference, I was using the Odin Infinity pad.

## Disappointment

Skates felt great for a couple weeks until one day it felt like I was dragging my mouse through mud. Aiming was impossible and I could not for the life of me figure out what was causing it. I washed my mousepad, cleaned the skates, and tried different mice on the pad. Turns out, the glass skates were literally destroying the cloth pad it was on and I had to buy a completely new pad. Do not even think of putting these on glass pads as glass on glass is generally not a good idea.

## Conclusion

I would never recommend this to anyone. If you're looking for smooth glide and less friction, either go for glass pads or get good aftermarket PTFE skates. These are not it and will rip through your pad in a couple weeks. This advice also goes for different types of hard skates like sapphire skates. Just stick to PTFE or UHMWPE if you're on glass.
