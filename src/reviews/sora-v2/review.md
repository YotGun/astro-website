# Ninjutso Sora V2

The Sora V2 was my first lightweight mouse with it being sub _40g_. The mouse felt very solid with crisp clicks which was surprising for its weight. This mouse supports higher polling rates but only with another dongle that's sold seperately. In my opinion, 1k polling is sufficient even for the highest levels of play and with the longer battery life, I don't see any reason for me to buy the dongle. The scrollwheel is average but is a little bit harder to click. Side buttons are perfectly usable either for productivity or as keybinds ingame.

## Specs:

- Weight: ~39g
- Sensor: AIMNINJA Optical Sensor (PAW 3395)
- Switches: Omron Optical
- Polling Rate: 1k / 8k with SnappyFire dongle

## Creaking

After a couple months of using this mouse, the body has started creaking when force is applied to the sides. This does not compromise the mouse in any means and you would never experience this in normal gameplay unless you explicitly try to squeeze it. It is a little bit annoying but everything else about the mouse is still working flawlessly.

## Conclusion

With a lot of new mouse coming out in the same weight range with the same build quality and lower prices, I would not recommend this mouse to anyone. This mouse was ahead of its time and that is why I gave it a rating of 7.5. You would be better off checking out the newer Sora V3 or the ROG Harpe Ace Mini if you prefer more mainstream brands.
