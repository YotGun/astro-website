# XENNS Mangird Tea Pro

I've used headphones for gaming my entire life until I finally switched to my first pair of IEMS, the Tea Pro. Instead of going for entry level IEMs, I decided to just go for a premium one and break the bank.

# Specs

- Drivers: 2 DD + 6 BA
- Impedence: 13 ohms
- Connector: 0.78mm 2-pin
- Material: Aluminum body w/ resin shell
- Nozzle Diameter: 5.8mm
