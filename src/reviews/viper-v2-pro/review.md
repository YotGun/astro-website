# Razer Viper V2 Pro

Coming from the Razer Viper Ultimate, the Viper V2 Pro was a massive upgrade in almost every way. Its weight was slashed to _58g_ from _74g_. 16 grams might not sound like much but it feels like a whole different world on your fingers.

## Specs

- Weight: ~58g
- Sensor: Razer Focus Pro 30k (PAW 3950)
- Switches: Razer Optical Gen 3
- Polling Rate: 1k / 8k with Hyperpolling dongle

## First Impressions

This mouse felt great from the beginning. Removing the side rubber grips from the original Ultimate was a good choice and made the mouse feel a little bit more premium. Clicks are marginally improved with the new optical switches and the lighter weight made longer sessions less fatiguing. Side buttons are not bad but it has a little bit too much pretravel for my taste.

## Synapse

Razer Synapse might be the worst gaming peripheral software to have ever been made. It genuinely acts like a virus and is extremely invasive. The only reason to keep this software on your pc is: _Hypershift_. Essentially, Hypershift gives your mouse extra buttons by acting like a function layer on keyboards. While holding the assigned Hypershift button, every other button now acts as its own new button, doubling the amount of buttons available to you. This was a killer feature and I still miss it to this day. I hope other manufacturers implement this feature but I do not know how feasible it is especially in the day of web drivers.

## Scroll Wheel

The scroll wheel slowly gave out after using the mouse for a while. It was so infuriating that I swore to never buy another Razer mouse ever again. Everytime I scroll with this mouse, it would always jump to the opposite side. Only possible solutions that do not involve disassembling the mouse were only temporary. A year into using this mouse and the scroll wheel is basically already out of service.

## Conclusion

This mouse would have been a great mouse if not for the faulty scroll wheel. And this is not just my own unit either. Lots of people online seem to be having the same issue and only resolved it by replacing the scroll encoder with an optical encoder. I do not think it was worth it for me to do that and I was due for an upgrade anyways. The mouse did start creaking however only when youre intentionally pressing the body where you normally wouldn't ingame.
