# Pulsar Superglide Glass Mousepad

This superglide pad was my second experience with a hardpad, but first with glass. I've used plastic pads before but that was a complete waste of money since it basically wore down in a couple days. This one was different and was something I've been looking for all this time.

## First Impressions

Unboxing a glass pad for the first time is a little bit scary. This product feels fragile but is actually extremely solid. Using the pad felt awkward with how cold it is to the touch but the glide's unbeatable. Instantly my performance was better in faster paced high ttk games such as: Apex Legends, The Finals, Fortnite. The ability to just whip around your mouse without anything restricting it felt pretty amazing. However, I probably lost a couple ranks in VALORANT due to struggling to adapt to the low friction.

## Pros and Cons

Pros:

- Extremely durable, will last you years easily
- Very consistent glide profile (No slow spots ever)
- Easy to clean with just water and cloth
- Non-fatiguing especially in longer sessions
- Easiest glass pad to transition to since this pad is a little bit more controlled

Cons:

- The hardness might be uncomfortable to some
- Arm sleeves might be needed if you're particularly sweaty
- Hard to carry around if you travel a lot
- The smallest speck of dust or hair will cause loud scraping noise

# Conclusion

Even though this pad is relatively high maintenance (needing to wipe it before and during every session), it is worth it to me since I appreciate the consistency it provides. I am mainly a wrist aimer with high sensitivity and cloth pads lasts me a maximum of a couple months due to slow spots in the middle. Glass pads are also just generally cleaner. No dead skin cells or dust will be trapped in it and a simple wipe with a damp cloth or tissue will completely clean it. I would for sure recommend this pad to anyone who's looking to switch to glass pads. Consider looking into the version 3 of this pad since that will be thinner and more comfortable to game on.
